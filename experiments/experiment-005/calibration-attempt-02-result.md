# Calibration Attempt 02 Result: Failed Exact-Recovery Gate

## Decision

**Stopped before the 1,600-injection calibration matrix and before real data.**

Attempt 02 introduced an independent four-parameter logistic loss-cone
continuum at every energy row, sharing only beam energy, amplitude, and pitch
width. The model was intended to absorb broad pitch-dependent structure without
allowing loss-cone boundaries to vote on voltage.

## Exact Synthetic Test

A noiseless matched simulation used:

- beam center `150 eV`;
- amplitude `8`;
- pitch sigma `25 deg`;
- field ratio `0.975`;
- cone floor `0.2`;
- logistic boundary width `8 deg`;
- eight energy rows and 32 reflected pitches per row.

Under the frozen six-start development test, the practical estimator returned:

| Quantity | Truth | Practical fit |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Beam center | `150.0 eV` | `139.269 eV` |
| Amplitude | `8.0` | `6.929` |
| Pitch sigma | `25.0 deg` | `24.855 deg` |
| Log-residual RMS | `0` | `0.00862` |

The center error is `-10.73 eV` despite no noise and an exactly matched model.
A diagnostic initialized at the true beam and continuum parameters recovers the
truth with objective below `1e-12`. The forward model therefore contains the
correct solution, but its practical objective has a competing basin in which
independent row continua absorb part of the beam's energy response.

Fitting starting continua outside the field-aligned core and subtracting
candidate beams before continuum initialization did not remove the competing
solution. Sparse Jacobians made the computation feasible but did not change the
scientific failure.

## Meaning

The fully independent continuum is too flexible for a reliable operational
estimator. Even when beam and background are generated by the exact fitted
family, the nuisance parameters can trade against beam amplitude from row to row
and shift the inferred energy. A truth-dependent initialization can find the
global solution, but that is unavailable on real data and cannot be used as a
validation strategy.

Attempt 02 therefore fails before uncertainty calibration. Its split-calibration
protocol was not executed, no Attempt 02 acceptance rates were generated, and
the 398-sweep pilot remains closed.

The next candidate should constrain continuum variation smoothly across log
energy without imposing the adiabatic voltage equation. A low-dimensional
smooth logistic continuum is preferable to arbitrary independent row nuisance
parameters.
