# Experiment 003: Observation and Uncertainty Model Comparison

**Status:** Running; protocol and panel frozen

**Branch:** `experiments`

**Depends on:** [Experiment 001](../experiment-001/README.md) and
[Experiment 002](../experiment-002/README.md)

## Scientific Question

Which observation and uncertainty model best preserves known loss-cone physics,
fits real LP ER spectra without false precision, and yields empirically
calibrated voltage identifiability?

## Frozen Comparison

Candidate models were finalized from Experiment 002 before looking at the
confirmation panel:

1. **Raw interval count:** a negative-binomial interval likelihood for the
   Format-3 compression interval, with one profiled signal intensity per energy
   row, fixed 10 counts/s background, the Experiment-002 effective response,
   and one common quadratic overdispersion estimated on discovery spectra.
2. **Conditional shape:** a row-centered Student-t likelihood in log flux. One
   intensity per energy row cancels analytically; degrees of freedom are fixed
   at four and the robust residual scale is estimated on discovery spectra.
3. **Smooth Lillis:** the current masked linear objective with pairwise
   incident/reflected normalization. Its uncertainty is calibrated by matched
   simulation and residual/block resampling, not nominal Wilks thresholds.

A constant quasi-Poisson scale alone is not an adequate candidate because it
widens intervals without changing the biased Cash point estimate.

## Data Split

### Benchmarks

- Spectra 652, 653, and 654 from April 29, 1999.
- Published spectrum 653 is the primary positive control.

### Discovery Panel

Seven temporally spaced spectra were frozen without candidate results:
`635, 640, 645, 650, 655, 660, 665`. They span 40 minutes around the benchmark
and are used only to estimate the two discrepancy scales and diagnose code.

### Confirmation Panel

Spectra `669` through `718` form the fifty-sweep confirmation panel. They begin
after the Experiment-002 response-calibration interval (`638` through `668`),
so response validation and model confirmation are separated in time. Candidate
forms, masks, bounds, and tuning rules above are frozen before these fits.

The complete split, native timestamps, and raw block indices are tracked in
`panel_manifest.csv`, generated by `freeze_panel.py`.

## Common Physics And Bounds

All candidates use the same smooth loss-cone/beam forward model, fixed
`U_sc = +11 V`, 15 eV beam width, 7.5 degree beam pitch width, 5 degree boundary
smoothing, and the production Lillis mask. Fitted parameters and bounds are:

| Parameter | Bounds |
| --- | ---: |
| Lunar potential `U_M` | `[-2000, +20] V` |
| `B_S/B_M` | `[0.3, 1.1]` |
| Beam amplitude | `[0, 5]` |

The published primary target is `U_M = -160 V`, hence
`U_rel = U_M - U_sc = -171 V`, and `B_S/B_M = 0.975`. Preserving the benchmark
means `|U_rel + 171 V| <= 25 V`; this tolerance was fixed before candidate fits.

## Frozen Decision Metrics

- Primary benchmark voltage error and magnetic-ratio error.
- Median absolute standardized or scale-normalized residual.
- Boundary-hit and optimizer-seed disagreement rates.
- Empirical 68% and 95% interval coverage under matched simulation.
- Confirmation stability relative to smooth Lillis, summarized by median
  absolute voltage difference and catastrophic differences above 100 V.
- Residual structure by energy and reflected/incident side.

## Required Comparisons

- Benchmark voltage and magnetic-ratio recovery.
- Signed residual structure by energy and pitch.
- Parameter-bound and optimizer-failure rates.
- Sensitivity to normalization and mask definitions.
- Stability across optimizer seeds.
- Interval/profile resolution and boundary behavior.
- Repeated-noise coverage under matched and misspecified simulations.
- Effective information scale and residual overdispersion.

## Decision Gate

Advance one method only if it:

- Preserves the Halekas benchmark within a predeclared tolerance.
- Gives interpretable, non-structured residuals at its claimed uncertainty
  scale, or explicitly models remaining discrepancy.
- Avoids systematic voltage and nuisance-bound artifacts.
- Produces resolved intervals or diagnostics with demonstrated empirical
  calibration.
- Generalizes from discovery to confirmation spectra.

If no candidate passes, revise the forward model before uncertainty work.

## Non-Goals

- Mission-wide population statistics.
- Plasma-regime interpretation.
- Choosing the final 200 V or any other identifiability gate.
- Treating optimizer agreement as physical identifiability.
