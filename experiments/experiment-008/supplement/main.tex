\begin{abstract}
This prospective experiment tests which spatial and spatiotemporal potential
fields are identifiable from the corrected Lunar Prospector sampling operator
without using fitted potential values. The analysis treats each energy sweep as
one integrated observation, measures rank, conditioning, resolution, and
near-null structure, and performs frozen synthetic recovery tests. Scientific
results will be inserted only after the protocol and implementation are frozen.
\end{abstract}

\section{Background}
The manuscript poses global reconstruction as a spherical-harmonic inverse
problem. Sparse instantaneous longitude coverage and time-dependent plasma
conditions can confound spatial and temporal coefficients. Coverage plots and
regularized fits alone do not establish non-identifiability; the observation
operator itself must be audited.

\section{Observation Operator}
For sweep $i$, temporal basis $T_q$, and real spherical harmonic $Y_k$, the
canonical row is
\begin{equation}
A_{i,qk}=\frac{1}{n_i}\sum_{j\in i}T_q(t_{ij})Y_k(x_{ij}).
\end{equation}
This represents one sweep-level estimate assembled from fifteen energy rows.
Repeating the estimate at fifteen footprints would change its statistical
weight without adding independent information.

\section{Identifiability Diagnostics}
Let $A=U\Sigma V^T$. We report exact and effective rank, condition number,
truncated-SVD resolution, leverage, and the global visibility of the smallest
right-singular mode. A near-null witness is scaled to 100~V global RMS and
evaluated both globally and at the sampled footprints.

\section{Prospective Tests}
The primary model uses independent 24-hour windows and degree five. Frozen
coarser, static, and synodic-basis controls distinguish complete failure from
conditional coarse recovery. Within-basis and localized smooth injections test
recovery under fixed noise levels and blocked time/longitude validation.

\section{Limitations}
Geometry-only failure is an optimistic upper-bound result because later
measurement selection can only remove rows. Geometry-only success does not
validate the potential estimator, spacecraft-potential correction, or physical
interpretation.
