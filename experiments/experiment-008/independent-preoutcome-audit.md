# Independent Pre-outcome Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-13  
**Timing:** Geometry cache certified; no scientific spectrum or recovery result
computed

Two fresh agents independently reviewed the frozen protocol, new operator
helpers, and existing temporal implementation. They were instructed to attack
the experiment rather than confirm its expected conclusion.

## Shared Blocking Findings

Both reviews independently identified these issues:

1. empirical unit-column normalization can hide a physically invisible mode;
2. the row-level temporal product must be averaged exactly within a sweep;
3. underdetermined SVDs must expose actual null vectors rather than infer them;
4. window anchors, denominators, recovery fractions, and outcome precedence
   needed executable definitions;
5. unweighted equal-angle RMS is not a physical surface-area norm;
6. 32 replicates weakly determine a 90th-percentile recovery gate;
7. blocked mission-tail tests do not apply to independent-window coefficients;
8. sensitivity comparisons need native and paired common-sweep cohorts.

## Additional Findings

The audits also required an explicit spacecraft-potential gauge statement,
formal parity with the repository complex harmonic basis, exact versus
conditional interpretation of static recovery, shared sweep-level noise in the
legacy row-duplication sensitivity, clarification of MAG norm validity bounds,
and stable tall-skinny QR instead of Gram-derived high-order rank decisions.

## Resolution

[Protocol Amendment 03](protocol-amendment-03.md) resolves the blocking
findings prospectively. The implementation now includes:

- an analytic surface-area and space-time field metric;
- exact row-level temporal tensor accumulation;
- explicit underdetermined null vectors and zero-padded spectra;
- real/complex field and coefficient-norm parity tests;
- a gauge-safe contrast-only sensitivity;
- covariance-based recovery quantiles with retained frozen injections;
- model-appropriate blocked synthetic recovery;
- native and paired sensitivity cohorts;
- tall-skinny QR for large controls.

The full agent transcripts were conversational review artifacts and are not
treated as data. This document records their scientific findings and the
pre-outcome decisions they caused.
